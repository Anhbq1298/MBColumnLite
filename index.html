Uses the MBColumn Eurocode 2 fibre PMM solver (γc 1.5, γs 1.15, εud 0.045; concrete fibres 100 × 100 by default, 72 neutral-axis angles × 120 depths) and radial 3D D/C check; each capacity point and every point on the charts is then solved exactly on the strain plane (Newton), so results and curves do not depend on the mesh. Ported from the C# core and matched against its approved baselines. Slenderness (EC2 §5.8, nominal curvature) and the minimum moment N·e0 are applied when the slenderness check is on; otherwise demands are checked as entered. Preliminary study tool; results must be verified by a qualified engineer.
